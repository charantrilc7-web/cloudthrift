Security first

Built for teams who answer to auditors.

🔒
Strictly read-only

SpendSensai observes — it never creates, modifies, or deletes a single resource. Scoped Reader role only, revocable by you at any time.

🔑
Your keys, your data

Authentication happens directly with Microsoft. No passwords stored, cost data processed transiently and never resold.

🔑
Reader role only

You grant a scoped Azure Reader role. Creating, changing, or deleting anything is impossible — the permission itself doesn't allow it.

→
📈
Cost + metrics only

We read billing lines and utilization metrics. We never open the contents of your databases, storage, code, or secrets.

→
⛔
Revoke in one click

Remove the role assignment in the Azure portal whenever you want. Our access ends instantly — no offboarding, no lock-in.

What happens to your data. We process only cost and utilization metadata — never the contents of your resources. Data is analyzed transiently to produce your report, is never sold, shared, or used to train any model, and is deleted on request. Full detail in our privacy & data-handling policy.
